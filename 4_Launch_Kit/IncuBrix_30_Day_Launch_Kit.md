# IncuBrix 30-Day Social Launch Kit

## Executive summary

Prepare a proof-led content program for podcasters, YouTubers, expert-led
creators, editors, and small content teams. Publish across LinkedIn, Instagram,
YouTube Shorts, X, and Threads. Every post carries an asset-level UTM. The
first week establishes a baseline; all targets below are assumptions to reset
after real data exists.

## Audience and geography

Primary audience: solo and 1–5 person teams already producing long-form audio
or video at least monthly and struggling to repurpose it consistently. Buyers
and influencers include creators, editors, content managers, coaches, and
founders. Priority regions: United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Ireland,
Australia, New Zealand, and Singapore.

## Platform strategy

| Platform | Role | Why | Starting cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Primary | Expert creators, founders, and saveable carousels | 5/week |
| Instagram | Primary | Reels and carousel-native creator education | 4/week |
| YouTube Shorts | Primary | Natural extension of long-form back catalogues | 3/week |
| X | Experiment | Fast hook and copy testing | 5/week |
| Threads | Experiment | Low-production conversation and reposting | 3/week |

Keep the best two channels by qualified clicks per post after enough comparable
posts exist. Do not call a platform a failure from one post.

## Content pillars and voice

1. Hooks and openings — earn the first two seconds.
2. Captions and editing craft — trims, safe areas, and readable copy.
3. Repurposing playbooks — turn one source into several useful formats.
4. Creator workflow — batching, approval, measurement, and iteration.
5. Trust and rights — consent, source preservation, and transparent AI use.

Voice: practical, creator-to-creator, specific, short sentences, no guarantees,
no unsupported statistics, and one action per post. Product features remain
Draft until verified with evidence.

## Content-to-signup path

Useful post → one CTA → asset-specific UTM link → landing page → signup.
UTMs use `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, campaign, asset ID in
`utm_content`, and pillar in `utm_term`. Confirm that the signup system retains
these fields before claiming social-attributed signups.

## 30-day calendar

The complete 40-row draft calendar is `30_Day_Content_Calendar.csv`. It includes date,
platform, asset, pillar, hook, caption, CTA, link, time, owner, and target/actual
fields. It spans 2026-11-02 through 2026-12-01. Every row is pending human
rights, brand, editorial, account, and publication approval. Dates are a
planning template; the candidate must confirm the actual launch date.

## First Monday

1. 09:00 — confirm product/account access, owner, approver, and launch date.
2. 09:30 — verify product claims; unsupported claims remain Draft.
3. 10:30 — confirm landing-page UTM persistence with one test conversion.
4. 11:00 — inspect the first seven scheduled posts, media crops, and links.
5. 13:00 — approver records signoff; do not publish unauthorized branding.
6. Scheduled slot — publish only if the first asset is explicitly approved.
7. Within 24 hours — reply to genuine comments and record the public URL.

## Operating workflows

Daily: check due rows, preview the post, obtain approval, publish, verify the
link, respond within 24 hours, and log the URL. After a consistent 48-hour
window, record reach, interactions, clicks, and signups if attribution exists.

Weekly: run one new input, review claims/rights, approve the batch, compare
like-for-like posts, document keep/change/stop decisions, and update the next
week. Never delete weak results.

## Budget assumptions

Offline engine, fonts, and native scheduling: USD 0 direct run cost. Optional
paid test budget: USD 0 by default; up to USD 200 in weeks 3–4 only with written
approval and only on the best proven organic post. Candidate AI subscription
costs are pending disclosure.

## Planning targets

| Metric | 30-day assumption | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Posts | 40 delivered assets; scale toward 80 only if capacity permits | Public URLs |
| Reach | 40,000 total | Native impressions/views by asset |
| Median engagement | ≥3% primary channels | Consistent interactions ÷ impressions |
| Link clicks | 200 | UTM report by asset |
| Signups | 20 only if UTM capture works | Signup records retaining source/content |

These are forecasts, not results. Reset them after week-one baseline data.

## KPI review and reporting

Report posts, reach, median engagement, clicks/post, click-through rate, and
signups where attribution exists. Segment by platform, pillar, format, and hook.
Keep observation windows consistent. The public LinkedIn follower-based proxy
in the competitor audit is not a full engagement rate.

## Approval and compliance

Technical draft → growth review → claim check → rights/brand check → platform preview →
approver signoff → schedule → post verification. Required checks: source
traceability, no unsupported feature claim, no third-party media without
permission, no private data, appropriate AI label, correct crop/duration,
working UTM, authorized brand treatment, and one CTA.

## Risk register

| Risk | Owner | Mitigation | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Generic text-only creative | Growth | Add original screen/voice proof weekly | Two posts below baseline |
| Unsupported claim | Approver | Evidence-linked claim register and Draft hold | Any feature wording |
| Rights or privacy issue | Approver | Written consent/license and no private data | Missing evidence |
| UTM loss | Analytics | Test before launch; report clicks only if signup source is lost | Click with no attribution |
| Platform change | Growth | Recheck help guidance monthly | Crop/rejection warning |
| Low reach | Growth | Consistency, real replies, no purchased engagement | Week-one miss |
| Provider outage/unsafe AI | Engineer | Offline default and validation | AI error or failed trace |

## End-of-month decision

Continue channels with repeatable qualified clicks and manageable production;
change weak hooks/formats with a documented hypothesis; stop channels that show
repeatedly weak signal after comparable tests. Report uncertainty and all
results, including zeroes.
