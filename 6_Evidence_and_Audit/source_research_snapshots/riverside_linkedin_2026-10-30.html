Riverside

Video Editing Software

Riverside makes it easy to record remote podcasts and video interviews that look and sound like they were recorded in a million-dollar studio. Riverside enables anyone, from individual podcasters to enterprise brands and media companies, to record high quality content without depending on internet quality. Content is our thoughts, opinions, and ideas. You can change the world with content. Our mission is to empower creators and businesses to tell their stories and amplify their voices. With Riverside, you get: - Local recordings up to 4K video resolution - Uncompressed crystal clear audio - Separate audio and video tracks - Easy editing with our Magic Editor - Ability to generate clips easily - Mobile app to record from anywhere - And more And you can use Riverside to record: - Podcasts - Videos - Screen Shares - Presentations - Trainings - Executive Interviews - Webinars - Live streams - And more We’re excited to see what you create!

    I said a word wrong in our podcast and didn’t re-record it. Here’s a few things I really like about Riverside and wanted to share with you guys. We’ve been using it for a long time now, and at Concurate we use it for almost everything. We record our podcasts and meetings on it, edit the podcasts there, make the YouTube Shorts there, and upload from there too. You don’t have to jump between five different tools. The first thing I like is the recording quality. Most platforms just record the video call itself, so the quality depends on the internet. Riverside records directly on each person’s computer, using their own camera and mic, and then uploads that to the cloud. So even if someone’s internet is slow, the recording still comes out in high quality. The second thing is editing, which is where it saves us the most time. You can edit the video by editing the text. Delete a sentence in the transcript and it’s gone from the video. You can remove pauses and filler words like “um” and “uh” in one click, add captions automatically, and make the voice sound cleaner. My favorite feature is voice dubbing. Let’s say I said a word wrong in the middle of a podcast. Normally I’d have to record it again. Here, I select that part and it generates my voice saying the correct word. I use this a lot. For YouTube videos with slides, Riverside records the slides and the person separately, so while editing I can change the layout however I want. You’re not stuck with one fixed frame. I also added the Concurate logo, layout and style as brand guidelines once. Now I just tell Riverside to apply the brand, and it adds the logo, sets the layout, fills empty spaces, removes pauses, checks the framing and adds captions. Sharing for review is simple too. I send a link, and the person can comment right on the transcript, like “change this” or “cut this part.” No long feedback calls needed. We still use Premiere Pro and After Effects for heavy effects and final polish. But Riverside lets you export the edited project straight into Premiere, so we do all the quick work in Riverside first and only finish the polish in Premiere. The team is also very active. They keep releasing new features and posting tutorials on YouTube. Recently they launched four AI agents: Clipper, Marketer, Producer and Editor. I haven’t tried them yet, but I will soon. If you record podcasts or long videos and want to go from recording to publishing faster, Riverside is worth trying.
