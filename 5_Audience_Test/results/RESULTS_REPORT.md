# IncuBrix Audience Preference Test – Results

## 1. Study Overview

- Form submissions: 20
- Unique participant codes: 20
- Completed content-pair comparisons: 80
- Completed comparison sections: 4 per participant

## 2. Preference Results

| Question | Post 1 | Post 2 | No Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Would make me stop scrolling | 38.8% | 45.0% | 16.3% |
| Is clearer | 28.8% | 62.5% | 8.8% |
| Is more useful | 38.8% | 50.0% | 11.3% |

## 3. Product Interest

- Yes: 12 respondents
- Maybe: 7 respondents
- No: 1 respondent

19 out of 20 respondents indicated Yes or Maybe when asked whether they would try a content-generation tool like this.

## 4. Key Findings

- Post 2 showed the strongest preference for clarity.
- Post 2 also had a smaller advantage in stopping the scroll and perceived usefulness.
- Save/share ratings were relatively close between the two posts.
- The responses indicate encouraging initial interest in the proposed tool.

## 5. Methodology

The preference study was conducted using a Google Form with 20 submissions and 20 unique participant codes. The reported results are based on 80 completed content-pair comparisons.

## 6. Limitations

This is an exploratory preference study. The results should not be interpreted as statistically representative of the entire international creator market or as proof of actual product adoption.

No social-media publication or engagement performance is claimed.

## 7. Conclusion

The findings suggest that clear, understandable content should be prioritized in the IncuBrix social media content strategy. Further testing can help validate these initial preferences.
